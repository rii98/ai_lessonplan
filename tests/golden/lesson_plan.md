# Components of Environment: Biotic and Abiotic

**Curriculum:** CDC · Grade 6 · Science · SC6.2.1
**Duration:** 45 min · **Framework:** 5E · **Language:** en-ne
**Timing:** planned 45 min / target 45 min (✓ balanced)

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

1. Classify components into biotic and abiotic _(Bloom: understand)_
2. Identify producers, consumers, and decomposers _(Bloom: apply)_

## Previous Knowledge

- Living vs non-living things
- Examples from surroundings

## Engagement Hook

> What did you see on your way to school today?
> — _question_

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception:** Clouds move so they are living — **Correction:** Movement is not life; clouds are abiotic _(source: CDC Science 6, misconception bank)_

## Local Context

- paddy field
- goat
- mushroom
- river water
- sunlight

## Teaching Materials

- Component cards
- Board and marker
- Textbook

## 5E Lesson Plan

| Phase | Teacher Activities | Student Activities | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engage (संलग्न गराउनु) | • Ask what students saw on the way to school | • Share one observation | 10 min |
| Explore (अन्वेषण गर्नु) | • Give groups component cards to sort | • Sort cards and justify choices | 15 min |
| Explain (व्याख्या गर्नु) | • Introduce producers, consumers, decomposers | • Copy the concept map | 20 min |

## Differentiation

- **Struggling:** Provide labelled picture cards
- **On level:** Sort mixed cards
- **Advanced:** Build a food chain from the cards

## Evaluation Questions

1. Which of these is a biotic component?
2. A mushroom is a decomposer.
3. Name one producer found near your school.

## Homework

- Draw four things and label B/A
- Mark producers, consumers, decomposers
